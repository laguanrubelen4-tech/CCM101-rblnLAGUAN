Among AWS, Azure, and GCP, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was Microsoft Azure because of its strong connection with Microsoft technologies. I found it useful for organizations that already use Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory. Azure also provides many services that can support businesses of different sizes.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide essential cloud services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, security, and identity management. They also provide scalable infrastructure that allows businesses to adjust their resources based on their needs. However, each platform has different strengths and specialized services.

Business requirements greatly influence cloud platform selection because organizations have different budgets, technologies, workloads, and goals. For example, a Microsoft-based organization may prefer Azure, while an AI-focused company may choose GCP. A startup may select AWS because of its wide range of services and scalability.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me how to analyze business requirements before recommending a cloud platform. I learned that choosing a cloud provider requires considering cost, performance, scalability, security, and existing technologies. This activity helped me understand how cloud services can solve real-world business problems.
